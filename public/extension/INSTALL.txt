IronClad Ad Blocker
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions
3. Turn on Developer mode.
4. Choose Load unpacked and select this folder.
5. Click the toolbar shield. Use Zapper, or right-click any page and choose "IronClad Zapper — hide an element".
The badge counts blocked requests and pulses. Chrome only reports individual matches for unpacked extensions.
